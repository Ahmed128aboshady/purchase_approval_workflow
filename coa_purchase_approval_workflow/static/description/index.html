Eliminate Procurement Blindspots

Standard Odoo lacks multi-tier amount authorizations, SLA auto-escalation, and delegated authorities, exposing companies to procurement overspending.

Without Purchase Approval Workflow

  • Buyers confirm multimillion purchase orders with a single click without managerial authorization.
  • Requisitions stall when managers are on annual leave, causing severe operational bottlenecks.
  • No SLA deadlines or escalation, leading to missed vendor delivery windows and lost discounts.
  • Auditors cannot trace who authorized spending or why prices were modified after quotation.

With COA Purchase Approval Workflow

  • Dynamic spending thresholds route orders up to $10k to Leads, $50k to Directors, and above to CFOs.
  • Delegation & Out-of-Office modes automatically redirect pending approvals to deputies.
  • Automated SLA cron escalates overdue requests to superiors before deadlines expire.
  • Comprehensive audit trail exportable to Excel and branded PDF certificates.

Key Procurement Governance Features

A battle-tested procurement approval matrix engineered for medium to large enterprise compliance.

Dynamic Spending Tiers

Configure unlimited tiers per department or company. Set minimum amount limits, currency conversion, and specific approver roles per level.

SLA & Auto-Escalation

Define per-level response SLAs. If an approver fails to act within the window, the automated cron escalates the requisition to the next manager.

1-Click Email Approval

Approve or reject purchase requisitions directly from notification emails on mobile without logging into Odoo, using secure one-time tokens.

Out-of-Office Delegation

Heading on leave? Assign approval authority to a designated colleague with start and end dates. Zero operational downtime while maintaining audit trails.

Trusted Vendor Bypass

Mark certified partners as Trusted Vendors with low-value bypass limits to fast-track routine office supplies and recurring consumables.

Audit Export (Excel & PDF)

Export full historical logs of every submission, approval, refusal reason, SLA delay, and delegation to Excel or branded executive PDF sheets.

How the Purchase Approval Workflow Operates

Structured progression from RFQ drafting to final purchase confirmation.

Step 01
RFQ Creation

Buyer drafts a Purchase Order. System evaluates amount limits, category, and vendor rules.

Status: Draft / Matrix Attached
Step 02
Tiered Notification

Requisition locks. Level 1 approvers receive scheduled Odoo activities and tokenized email alerts.

Action: SLA Clock Active
Step 03
Sequential Sign-Off

Approvers validate via email or web. Refusals require documented feedback returned to the buyer.

Chatter: Full Time-Stamped Log
Step 04
Auto PO Confirmation

Upon reaching final tier sign-off, the order automatically confirms and generates incoming receipts.

Result: PO Confirmed & Dispatched

Frequently Asked Questions

Can approvers approve purchase orders from mobile email?

Yes. Every notification email contains encrypted, one-click Approve and Reject buttons that allow managers to take action directly from their smartphone without logging into Odoo.

How does the out-of-office delegation work?

Users can set a substitute delegate and specify a date range. Any purchase orders requiring their sign-off during this period will automatically route to the delegate with full audit trail logging.

Can we bypass approvals for trusted regular vendors?

Yes. Vendors can be flagged as Trusted with an optional maximum amount bypass limit, allowing routine purchasing to proceed without managerial interruption.
